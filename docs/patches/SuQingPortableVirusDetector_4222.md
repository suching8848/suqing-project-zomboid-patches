# 蘇清的便携病毒侦测器 | SuQing’s Portable Virus Detector

## 简体中文

0.1.0 适用 B42.22.2 单人档，Mod ID 为 `SuQingPortableVirusDetector_4222`，依赖现代枪械系统 `ModernFirearmsSystem`。

1. 随身携带一块电子腕表、一把可用的螺丝刀和一个 MFS 病毒侦测器 `Gunpart.HeartbeatSensorPlus`。
2. 右键电子表，选择 **安装病毒侦测器**。完成时消耗侦测器，保留原表和工具。安装状态随手表保存，换手腕后沿用。
3. 右键已装好的电子表，选择 **开启雷达** 或 **关闭雷达**。佩戴或随身携带均可使用。面板可拖动，显示 50 格内已加载的活僵尸。楼上蓝色，同层红色，楼下青色。

雷达开启时每 0.5 秒扫描一次。手表离开随身物品栏时自动关闭，重新进档默认关闭。电子表的时间和闹钟照常使用，枪载雷达独立控制。

游戏内验收时先中止一次安装，确认材料仍在。完成安装后确认只消耗一个侦测器。再依次验证右键开关、与枪载雷达同时开启、换手腕、放入外部容器和保存重进。

## English

Version 0.1.0 supports single-player Build 42.22.2. Mod ID `SuQingPortableVirusDetector_4222`. Requires `ModernFirearmsSystem`.

1. Carry a digital wristwatch, a working screwdriver and an MFS Virus Detector (`Gunpart.HeartbeatSensorPlus`).
2. Right-click the watch and choose **Install virus detector**. The action consumes the detector and retains the original watch and tool. The installation is saved on the watch and carries over when switching wrists.
3. Right-click the installed watch to turn its radar on or off. It works when worn or carried. Drag the panel to move it. It shows loaded living zombies within 50 tiles. Blue marks the floor above, red the same floor, and cyan the floor below. Colors apply to all higher and lower floors in range.

The panel scans every half second while on. It shuts off when the watch leaves your inventory and starts off after loading. Time and alarms still work, and gun-mounted radar keeps its own controls.

For an in-game check, cancel one installation and confirm all items remain. Finish the installation and confirm exactly one detector is used. Try the radar with a gun-mounted sensor active, switch wrists, put the watch into external storage, and save and reload.
