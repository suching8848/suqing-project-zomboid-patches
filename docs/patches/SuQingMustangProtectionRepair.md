# 蘇清的野马防护改装修复

SuQing's Mustang Protection Repair 1.0.0

## 中文

依赖 tsarslib 与 ATA_Mustang，适用42.21单人。打开车辆改装菜单，选中已安装且受损的防撞杠、窗户防护或车轮防护，使用修理按钮。每次恢复30耐久，最高100；需要焊接2级、完好可用的焊接面罩、对应材料1件（金属板；车轮防护为重型链节）和喷灯3单位燃料。人物走近后执行，材料及状态会再次核对。

不拆除或替换原部件，不清空车辆改装数据；符合原作别名关系的NIKKE Mustang可识别。普通Mustang引擎盖配方保护是另一个补丁。本补丁不提供联机支持，也不修复原作未登记的其他车型/防护。停用前等动作完成，正常保存退出，停用并重启；已修理耐久保留，不自动回退。不可同时加载手动版和工坊版同ID。

原始记录为9项Kahlua行为回归与2项真实改装菜单回归，另有原生动作普通100/瞬间1时长检查；本轮重跑另记。未见用户完成实际修理的确认。验收：受损部件修理一次，核对30耐久、材料和3燃料；缺材料/技能时不能修；保存重进核对原部件数据。

## English

Requires tsarslib and ATA_Mustang. B42.21 single-player. Open the vehicle tuning menu, select damaged installed bullbar/window/wheel protection and use Repair. Each action restores 30 condition up to 100. Requires Welding 2, a usable welding mask, one matching material (sheet metal; heavy chain link for wheel protection), and 3 blowtorch fuel units. The character approaches the vehicle and inputs/state are revalidated.

Original parts and tuning data remain. Compatible upstream NIKKE Mustang aliases are recognized. The Mustang hood recipe guard is a separate patch. Multiplayer and unregistered vehicles/protection are unsupported. Let actions finish, save/exit normally, disable and restart; repaired condition persists and is not automatically reverted. Keep one loading source per Mod ID.

Recorded checks include 9 Kahlua cases, 2 actual tuning-menu cases and native action timing 100/instant 1. Reruns are separate evidence. User confirmation of an actual repair is pending. Verify condition/material/fuel changes, missing-input refusal, and saving/reloading.
