[h2]选择与使用[/h2]
先启用原作，再启对应补丁；保留原相对顺序，不自动重排名单。同Mod ID只保留一份来源，避免工坊与旧本地副本重复。启用、停用或换来源前按需备份并正常退出游戏；移除时看该子项说明，不用覆盖存档代替卸载。
人物菜单需要本包XFACTION汉化。N&C状态面板只读显示，不是药物本体，不强制汉化。Erika修复适用于已知家具；DebugMenu保护仅在其启用时运行。MFS三项修复不是减重挂件或固定收购的强制前置；批量农耕筛选不是灌溉养护的前置。
作者此前反馈自己的单人配置正常，仅限当时配置与版本；不代表25项、全部组合或后续原作均已实测。They Knew文字项不在该正式档反馈范围内。先在测试档检查菜单、操作及保存重进，再用于长期存档。库存刷新优化不承诺帧率提升；联机和服务器未验证。原作更新后复核补丁适用版本。
[h2]Selection and use[/h2]
Enable originals before their patches and preserve existing relative order. Keep one source per Mod ID; avoid duplicate local/Workshop copies. Back up as needed and exit normally before changing mods. Read each entry's removal notes; overwriting a save is not an uninstall method.
Character Menu needs this item's XFACTION translation. The read-only N&C status panel is not the drug mod and does not require its translation. Erika fixes apply to known furniture; the DebugMenu guard runs only if DebugMenu is enabled. MFS fixes are not mandatory for Weight Charms/Fixed Buyback; Batch Farming filter is not required by Irrigation Care.
The author's earlier normal-play report covers their configuration and versions, not all 25 entries, every combination or later originals. The They Knew text entry was outside that report. Check menus, actions and save/reload in a test save first. No FPS gain is promised; multiplayer/servers are unverified. Recheck compatibility after original-mod updates.
